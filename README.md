# Mad Tower – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 17, 2026

## Overview

Mad Tower ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile game developed for iOS by Jamie Thomas. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use Mad Tower, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

By downloading or playing Mad Tower, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## Information We Collect

Mad Tower does not directly collect or store personally identifiable information on our own servers. However, the following categories of data may be collected automatically through Apple platform services and third-party SDKs integrated into the app:

**Collected via Google AdMob (advertising):**

- Device identifiers, including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) when you grant App Tracking Transparency permission
- IP address and coarse (country/region-level) location derived from it
- Device type, operating system version, and language settings
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, view-through events)
- Diagnostic data related to ad delivery

**Collected via Apple Game Center (if you sign in):**

- Your Game Center player ID, nickname, and gameplay achievements/leaderboard scores are handled by Apple. We do not access, store, or transmit Game Center account data ourselves; it is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

**Stored locally on your device only:**

- Game progress data such as high score, coin balance, daily streak, and unlocked block skins. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

In accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, Mad Tower will present a system prompt asking for your permission to track activity across other companies' apps and websites. This permission controls access to your IDFA.

- If you **allow** tracking, AdMob may use your IDFA to serve personalized ads and measure ad performance across apps.
- If you **deny** tracking, AdMob will serve non-personalized (contextual) ads, and your IDFA will not be accessed.

You can change this choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** on your device.

## Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mad Tower uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, which is how the app remains free to download and play. AdMob may collect and process data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your device settings, ATT choice, and regional consent requirements.

For users located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Mad Tower presents Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form on first launch, in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive. You may change your consent preferences at any time from within the app.

Google's data practices are governed by Google's own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from apps and sites that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Apple Game Center

Mad Tower supports Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Use of Game Center is optional. Game Center is an Apple service, and any personal information associated with it (such as your nickname or player ID) is collected and processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## How We Use Information

Data collected through the third-party services described above is used solely for the following purposes:

- Displaying advertisements within the app (personalized or non-personalized, depending on consent)
- Measuring ad performance, reach, and fraud prevention
- Diagnosing crashes and improving app stability and performance
- Supporting platform features you choose to use (e.g., Game Center leaderboards)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your personal information with any third party outside of the services described in this policy.

## Children's Privacy

Mad Tower is rated 4+ in the App Store, but it is a general-audience game and is **not directed at children under the age of 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the user's jurisdiction).

In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU GDPR provisions for minors, and Apple's App Store guidelines, AdMob is configured to request non-personalized ads in contexts where the user may be a child.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to address your concerns.

## Data Retention and Security

We do not maintain servers or databases containing your personal data. All game progress (scores, coins, unlocked skins, streaks) is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Data collected by third-party SDKs (Google AdMob, Apple Game Center) is retained according to their own retention policies. We encourage you to review their privacy policies for details.

## Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding data processed by the third-party services we integrate:

- **Access, correction, deletion**, and **portability** of data processed about you (GDPR / UK GDPR)
- **Right to opt out of the sale or sharing** of personal information (California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA)
- **Right to limit use of sensitive personal information**

You can exercise the following controls directly on your device or with the relevant third-party provider:

- **Limit ad tracking** via *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*
- **Reset your Advertising Identifier** via *Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising*
- **Withdraw or change EU consent** by re-opening the in-app consent form (where available)
- **Delete all local game data** by uninstalling the app
- **Request information** from Google about data processed through AdMob via Google's privacy controls

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, applicable law, or industry best practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will indicate when the most recent revision was made. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our data practices, please reach out via Mad Tower Support. We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 30 days.
