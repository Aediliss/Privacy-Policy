PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: May 11, 2025
OVERVIEW

Mad Tower ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile game developed for iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use Mad Tower and how it is used.

By downloading or playing Mad Tower, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Mad Tower does not directly collect any personally identifiable information. However, the following data may be collected automatically through third-party services integrated into the app:

Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID / IDFA)
IP address and general location (country/region level)
Device type, OS version, and language settings
App usage data (session length, in-game events)
Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
Game progress data (high score, coin balance, daily streak, unlocked skins) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.
THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

Mad Tower uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your device settings and consent choices.

Google's data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy:

Google Privacy Policy
How Google uses data from apps
You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS.
HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Data collected through third-party services is used solely for the following purposes:

Displaying advertisements within the app
Measuring ad performance and reach
Improving app stability and performance
We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with any third party outside of the services described in this policy.
CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Mad Tower is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

AdMob is configured to serve non-personalized ads only in contexts where the user may be a child, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store guidelines.

If you believe your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove that information.
DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

We do not maintain servers or databases containing your personal data. All game data (scores, coins, unlocks) is stored locally on your device and can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app.

Data collected by third-party services (AdMob) is retained according to their own policies. We encourage you to review Google's privacy policy for details.
YOUR RIGHTS & CHOICES

Limit ad tracking via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
Reset your Advertising ID via Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising
Delete all local game data by uninstalling the app
Contact us to request information about data collected by third parties on our behalf
